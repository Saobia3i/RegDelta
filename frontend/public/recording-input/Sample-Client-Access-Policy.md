# SAMPLE — Client Access and Identity Policy

This synthetic policy is provided for the RegDelta walkthrough. It is not a real company's policy and must not be treated as evidence for an actual client.

## Account ownership
The IT administrator creates a named account for each employee after the employee's manager approves the request. Shared employee accounts are prohibited. The manager reviews employee access every quarter.

## Access changes and termination
Managers request access changes when an employee changes roles. IT disables an employee account within one business day after notice of termination. Exceptions are recorded by the IT administrator.

## Authentication
Multi-factor authentication is required for remote access and administrator accounts. Passwords must be changed after suspected compromise. The policy does not currently define ownership, review, or lifecycle requirements for service accounts or hardware identities.

## Review gap
Service accounts and device identities are maintained by technical teams; the policy does not require a named owner, periodic access review, or documented revocation for those identities.
